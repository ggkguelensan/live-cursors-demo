# Third-party sources

The generated optional Reflect operator in `src/vendor/reflect` comes from the
user-owned [Reflect fork](https://github.com/ggkguelensan/reflect), licensed MIT as
declared by that package. Every generated source file identifies its immutable
source URL. `scripts/vendor-reflect.mjs` is the single revision source.

DM Sans and Lora are self-hosted through their Fontsource packages, under the
SIL Open Font License. Their license texts are shipped in `public/licenses`.
Avatars are original SVG source in this repository; no remote image service is used.
Other dependencies retain their respective npm package licenses.
